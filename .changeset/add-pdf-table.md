Add `PdfTable`, `PdfTableHeader`, `PdfTableRow` and `PdfTableCell`, with `'auto'` columns, `colspan` and `rowspan`

The header repeats on every page of the table; a header that would end a column alone moves to the next column. A row that splits across pages keeps each cell in its column on both pages, and a cell whose lines fit stays on its page. A column is a share (a number), a width with a unit or a percentage (`'80pt'`, `'30%'`), `'auto'` (as wide as its widest cell on one line; share columns take the rest) or an object `{ width, align, style }` whose `align` and `style` apply to each cell that starts in the column. `colspan` and `rowspan` on `PdfTableCell` span columns and rows. A table whose columns need more room than it has to keep their longest words whole gives the layout warning `PDF_TABLE_COLUMNS_TOO_NARROW`.

A cell is as wide as its columns, so the column decides: a `width`, `minWidth` or `maxWidth` on a cell fails with `PDF_TREE_INVALID` unless every column it covers is `'auto'`, a horizontal margin narrows the cell inside its column, and a percentage or `'auto'` horizontal margin, `flex`, `flexBasis`, `flexGrow`, `flexShrink` and `position: 'absolute'` on a cell fail with `PDF_TREE_INVALID`. A cell that spans percentage and length columns rejects a right margin (`margin`, `marginHorizontal`, `marginRight`). A row or cell directly inside a cell fails (put a `PdfTable` in the cell), errors name the table component, and a static `colspan="2"` fails with the hint to bind it as `:colspan="2"`. A `PdfTableCell` always renders a `PdfView`: a cell with only text puts it in one `PdfText` child, so `align` and `justifyContent` act the same whatever the content, and that text takes `maxLines`, `textOverflow`, `textDecorationColor` and `textDecorationStyle` from the cell's style. New exported types: `PdfTableProps`, `PdfTableHeaderProps`, `PdfTableRowProps`, `PdfTableCellProps`, `PdfTableColumn`, `PdfTableColumnWidth` and `PdfTableColumnOptions`.
