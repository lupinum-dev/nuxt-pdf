Fix page breaks that lost or cut content, squeezed lines, added empty pages or ran until the time limit

- Text is no longer lost at a page end: in a box with top padding inside another box, as the last line of a text with a top padding or border (such as a table cell), or in a text with fewer lines than `orphans`, which now moves to the next page whole.
- A `break` on the first element of a page no longer adds an empty page before it. A box that ends less than 0.001 pt past the page end stays on its page. A view whose content ends on a page, and of which only the bottom padding, border or margin crosses the page end, adds no empty part (or only its fixed children, such as a table header) to the next page.
- Lines are no longer squeezed: on a page with a page number, above a trailing `fixed` node (such as a `fixed` row written after the rows of a table, for which the flow now breaks early enough), on a page with `flexDirection: 'column-reverse'` and a fixed node, and in a `PdfText` with a `minHeight` that breaks, whose next part keeps the rest of the `minHeight`, as a view does. A text with a page number that starts at a page end is painted once.
- The parts of a box that pagination splits lose the edges the split removes: the lines that continue on the next page no longer start below the top padding, and the part before no longer keeps the bottom padding and margin. A view with a percentage `minHeight` near the page end splits there, and its next part keeps the rest.
- A node that cannot split in a row that splits, such as an image, an SVG or a `wrap={false}` view, is drawn once at its own size: it moves to the next page or, when it is higher than the page, stays on its own page. A row whose first cell with content does not fit moves whole, so its later cells no longer start a page before the first one.
- A view around a node no longer changes where the node starts on the next page: a text that `orphans` moves keeps its top margin, and a node inside a view whose bottom margin does not fit moves to the next page. After an automatic page break, a negative top margin pulls a node no higher than the top of the content area.
- An absolute node that `top` places below the first page of its parent prints on the page where its offset lands, and percentage insets and offsets take the whole parent's height. A full-page absolute background, such as an SVG a little higher than the page, prints on the page of the content, with no `PDF_UNBREAKABLE_NODE_OVERFLOW` warning, and a box exactly as high as the content area gives no warning either.
- A `fixed` node declared after a block that cannot split and is higher than the page, such as a footer written last, also repeats on that page. A row with a `fixed` node repeats it beside the row's content on each of its pages.
- A document with a `PdfNote` in the flow breaks pages by the same rules as every other document.
- Pagination that cannot finish fails at once instead of running until the time limit or out of memory: two pages in a row that take no content fail with `PDF_LAYOUT_ERROR` ("Pagination cannot end"); `fixed` content at least as high as the content area fails with `PDF_LAYOUT_ERROR` and names the node, its height and the room on the page; `limits.maxPages` stops pagination as it starts the first page past the limit; a text whose first lines are higher than the page keeps at least one line on each page; and builds that render in-process check the time limit once per page.
- Long documents render faster and with less memory, with the same pages: pagination lays out each rest of a page only down to one page below its end, and long views with a `minHeight` no longer take time that grows with the square of their length.
