Add tagged PDF output for PDF/UA-1 and PDF/A-3a with the `tag`, `alt`, `decorative` and `header` props

`conformance` on `PdfDocument` now takes `'PDF/UA-1'` and `'PDF/A-3a'` next to `'PDF/A-3b'`, or a list of them with at most one PDF/A level, such as `['PDF/A-3a', 'PDF/UA-1']`. Both levels write a structure tree in template order, which veraPDF validates. `tag` on `PdfText` marks headings (`h1` to `h6`) and paragraphs; `tag` on `PdfView` marks a `section`, `list`, `list-item`, `quote`, `caption` or `figure` (a figure view with `alt` holds its images, texts and caption as one figure); `alt` on `PdfImage` and `PdfSvg` describes the image; `decorative` makes a node an artifact that assistive technology skips. `header` on `PdfTableCell` makes a body cell the header of its row. Paragraphs, tables (header cells, column spans, a repeated header tagged once), links and images inside text are tagged without props, and `fixed` content is an artifact. A text with `textTransform` or `letterSpacing` carries its template text as `ActualText`, so assistive technology reads "From the managing director", not the painted capitals.

A tagged document fails with `PDF_TREE_INVALID` and the source position when it has no `language` (and no `title` for PDF/UA-1), an image without `alt` or `decorative`, a first heading other than `h1` or a skipped heading level, a text in a standard font, a list item outside a list, a link in a `fixed` or `decorative` node, a `PdfNote`, a figure view without `alt`, a link, table or `tag` inside a figure outside its caption, or a table or `tag` inside a `PdfLink` around blocks (a link is inline content; put it inside the blocks). Each `PdfLink` is its own link element, also next to a link with the same `href`, and the boxes of `debug` are artifacts. `alt` on a `PdfView` without `tag="figure"` fails in every document. Without a tagged level the new props change nothing and the PDF keeps its bytes. The `conformance` error message now lists the three levels and "at most one PDF/A level". New exported types: `PdfTextTag` and `PdfViewTag`.
